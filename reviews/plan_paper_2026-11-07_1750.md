## Phase 1: Method (code and tests)

1. **S2, one constraint, on the node of the next input** (W3, W4-ii)
   - `MessagePlanner` gets `constraint_nodes = "next" | "all"`:
     - "next": the multiplier weights the information term of slice 0 only, and the root is solved on the first input of the K-step plan;
     - "all": the earlier shared multiplier, solved on the one-step program.
   - Fixed multipliers keep weighting every slice, so that γ = 1 stays EFE.
   - The root search expands its bracket instead of truncating at γ_max. A safety cap is kept and counted.
2. **S1, noise-consistent floor** (W1, W3)
   - `information_floor_type = "predictive"`: β = ½ ln|(R + Σ̄)(R + S_*)⁻¹|.
   - Unit tests:
     - the closed form on the integrator;
     - β < I^max for Σ^θ ∝ R;
     - the switch for one output coincides with the task floor;
     - the next-node planner only weights slice 0.
3. **S3:** the information of the applied mode is nondecreasing in γ. Take the smallest root, and return the feasible end at a jump. Text only.

## Phase 2: Experiments

4. **Integrator:** CBFE with S1 + S2 on the test seeds. Ablation of S1 and S2 separately (four CBFE variants).
5. **Arm:** CBFE with S1 + S2, known and unknown polarity, 10 seeds. Report the share of active steps, the multipliers (median, max, cap hits) and the time per step.
6. **Like-for-like baselines** (W2, W8, C2):
   - The dual MPC with the control prior as its input cost (`input_cost="beta"`) minimises exactly twice the negative log-posterior of CBFE with γ = ρ/2 on every node.
   - Tune ρ on held-out seeds:
     - integrator: over all priors, and per prior for the sensitivity curve;
     - arm: separately under known and unknown polarity.
7. **Probing-weight sensitivity** (P1.2): RMSE against ρ on the test seeds, per prior mean (integrator) and per condition (arm), with CBFE as a reference. This is the direct test of "the goal sets the weight".
8. **Statistics** (C4, P2.1): paired bootstrap intervals for both examples, written by one analysis script per experiment.

**Decision rule:** S1 and S2 are adopted if CBFE still regulates the arm under unknown polarity. Otherwise, keep the task floor and state that γ_max is the probing weight at saturation.
